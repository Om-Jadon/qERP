OTP from Gmail (optional)

Open Gmail in the account that receives the ERP OTP and paste its address-bar URL. The /u/0/ part tells the extension which account to read.

Asks the browser for Gmail access only when you tick this; unticking removes it. After sending the OTP it watches Gmail's unread-mail feed for 1 minute (no Gmail tab needed), keeps only the ERP OTP email, and sends nothing anywhere else.

Stored only in this browser's extension storage, unencrypted.
